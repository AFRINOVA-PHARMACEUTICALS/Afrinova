Who we are

Medicines made closer to the people who need them

Afrinova Pharmaceuticals Limited is dedicated to bringing high-quality, affordable medicines closer to the people who need them. We focus on treatments for non-communicable diseases and essential injectable medicines, including products that prevent and manage postpartum hemorrhage during childbirth.

Starting in Eastern and Southern Africa, Afrinova aims to expand across the continent, reducing Africa's reliance on imported pharmaceuticals while strengthening access to essential healthcare.

What we will make

Three areas where access saves lives

Our product portfolio is built around the treatments that African patients and health systems need most.

Non-communicable diseases

Affordable, quality treatments for chronic conditions that affect millions of families across the continent.

Essential injectables

Reliable local supply of the injectable medicines that patients, hospitals and clinics depend on every day.

Maternal health

Products that prevent and manage postpartum hemorrhage, helping to reduce maternal death rates in Africa so more mothers come home safely after childbirth.

Where we are headed

Our vision and mission

Vision

To become a trusted and competitive African pharmaceutical leader, delivering innovative, locally manufactured medicines while advancing health security and sustainable value creation across the continent.

Mission

To manufacture and deliver safe, effective, and affordable medicines through quality-driven production, strategic partnerships, and disciplined investment; expand access to priority treatments; develop local talent and supply chains; and build a resilient pharmaceutical enterprise that creates sustainable value for patients, partners, investors, and communities.

How we will get there

Strategic objectives

A clear, disciplined plan to build African pharmaceutical capacity that lasts.

  • Modern manufacturing

    Establish modern, scalable, and quality-compliant pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities in Kenya and Tanzania.

  • A focused portfolio

    Build a commercially viable product portfolio focused on non-communicable diseases and essential injectable medicines.

  • Approvals and market access

    Secure regulatory approvals and expand market access across Eastern and Southern Africa, followed by broader continental growth.

  • Strategic partnerships

    Develop financing, technology, distribution, and public-sector partnerships that accelerate scale and reduce execution risk.
